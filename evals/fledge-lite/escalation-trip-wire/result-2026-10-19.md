## The finding this run produced

Criterion 5 was written assuming an escalation always has a `PLAN.md` to hand over. The WITH arm
showed that is wrong, and that **the skill was wrong with it**:

> The wires trip *before* a `PLAN.md` exists, so there is no seed to hand over. The escalation is
> to plain `/fledge:fledge` (ingest → plan), **not** `--from=plan`.

The trip-wire check runs at step 3 *before* the plan is written, which is the common case — so the
skill's blanket instruction to "offer `/fledge:fledge --from=plan` with the lite `PLAN.md` as the
seed" would have had the agent either reference a file that does not exist or, worse, write a plan
purely to have something to hand over, having just concluded it should not be writing that plan.

**Both were fixed after this run:**
- `skills/fledge-lite/SKILL.md` § Escalation trip-wires now splits the offer by what exists —
  `--from=plan` with a seed, plain `/fledge:fledge` without, and an explicit "do not invent one".
- Criterion 5 in the fixture now scores the *correct* handoff rather than assuming a seed.

The WITH arm had already behaved correctly; the skill's text was what needed to catch up.

## Delta

- **Fixed by the skill:** criterion 4, the one that matters. The baseline reached the right verdict
  and then proceeded on its own authority; the WITH arm stopped at a gate and put the choice to the
  user, with lite's counter at abort stated as **0 spawns, 0 checkpoints**.
- **Not fixed:** none.
- **New rationalizations observed:** none. The WITH arm instead matched the ticket's own last line
  to the rationalization list: *"Small change — one guard in the send path plus a column"* against
  *"It's one guard plus a column." A column is a migration.* It called the ticket's self-estimate
  "the thing the trip-wire list exists to overrule, not evidence against it" — the intended read.

## The honest caveat

**The WITHOUT arm named four trip-wires with no lite skill present**, citing risk level 5, the
migration rule, P12 and C11 from the working agreements and the checklist. So this fixture is not
evidence that the skill supplies the *knowledge* to escalate — that knowledge is already in the
written standards, and a careful agent reaches it unprompted.

What the skill supplies is the **gate**: a fixed check-point, a defined action (stop and ask), and
a refusal to let "be careful" substitute for it — at the one moment the agent has an incentive to
skip the check, which is after it has already committed to the fast path. The baseline had no such
incentive, because it had no fast path to protect. That asymmetry is exactly why this fixture is
scored as a guard and not as a delta, and why it must be re-run on every `fledge-lite` change.

## Verdict

WITH arm passes 5/5 and found a defect in the skill, now fixed. Keep as the standing REFACTOR
guard; always run alongside `small-ticket-overhead`, which must still pass 10/10.
