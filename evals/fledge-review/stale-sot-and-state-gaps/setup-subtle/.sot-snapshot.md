# Back-to-back reservation design
*Last edited 2026-08-17 by Jordan Avery*

## Problem
Guests with consecutive reservations ("back-to-back": same guest, same hotel, departure of one
equals arrival of the next) receive every Guest Journey message twice.

## Design
- A pair is two consecutive reservations. The first is the **owner**.
- Add `Reservation.b2b_owner` (nullable FK to `Reservation`), set on the second leg of a pair.
- At **scheduling time**, if a reservation has a `b2b_owner`, do not schedule its pre-arrival or
  check-in messages.
- Recompute `b2b_owner` on PMS reservation update.

## Scope
- Pre-arrival and check-in messages only.
- Pairs only. Chains of three or more are rare; out of scope for the MVP.
